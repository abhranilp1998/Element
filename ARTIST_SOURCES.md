- https://abhranilp1998.github.io/Element/index.html identifies ICARUS with Kolkata,
  boom bap and trap, and supplies the Katana Freestyle (1817663250), Sesh Theke
  Suru (1817645550), and Chick Chai (1817689044) SoundCloud embeds and permalinks.
- https://www.instagram.com/markthename_icarus/ identifies Aritra Sarkar, aka
  L Shady, as a rapper, lyricist and producer. The biography is paraphrased;
  unsupported superlatives and time-sensitive follower counts are omitted.
- https://linktr.ee/MarkthenameIcarus supplies the direct destinations recorded in
  assets/artist-links.json. Spotify, both release drawers and their additional
  service lists were inspected. Artist-name collisions on Spotify mean the
  provided artist destination is retained without importing unrelated catalog data.

## On-site links hub

links.html reproduces the useful destination hierarchy directly in the website:
profiles, release-specific platform choices, videos and additional links. Action
buttons go directly to the selected service, not to Linktree. Affiliate/tracking
parameters were removed where the destination's stable identifier suffices.
The source's Omegle label is retained with an explicit CooMeet destination note.
External services may require sign-in or offer region-dependent playback.
